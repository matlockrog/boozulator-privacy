Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Boozulator ("the App") is developed by an independent developer. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains what information the App does and does not collect.

Information We Do Not Collect

Boozulator does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App does not require an account, login, or registration. All cocktail recipes and calculations are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any server.

Advertising

The free version of Boozulator displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain data to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location (derived from IP address)
  • App usage and interaction data

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. The App developer does not have access to any personally identifiable information collected by AdMob.

Users who purchase Boozulator Pro will have all advertisements removed, and no ad-related data collection will occur.

In-App Purchases

Boozulator offers an optional in-app purchase ("Boozulator Pro") processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The App developer does not collect or have access to any payment or billing information. All purchase data is handled by Apple in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

All user-created data, including saved cocktail recipes, is stored locally on your device. This data is not backed up to any external server operated by the developer. Standard iCloud or device backups configured by the user may include this data as part of the overall device backup.

Children's Privacy

Boozulator is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 17. The App does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
